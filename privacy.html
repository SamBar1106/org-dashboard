Sam ERP Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Sam ERP is a private internal tool operated by Samuel Barrios for his own business. It is not offered to the public.

Google data. Sam ERP uses Google Drive access (the drive.file scope) only to upload and manage backup files it creates itself in the owner's own Google Drive. It does not read, share or sell any other Drive files or Google user data.

Storage. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's private server, readable only by the app. No data is shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removal. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's Third-party access page.
